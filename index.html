Tropical Cyclone Track & Wind-Field Explorer

Pick a year and storm to plot its best-track. The track is coloured by Saffir–Simpson category; shaded areas show the 34 kt (tropical-storm-force) and 64 kt (hurricane-force) wind radii at each time. Click any track point to read its date, position, intensity and size. Source: NOAA IBTrACS v04r01 — all global basins, using the US agency for each (NHC in the Atlantic & E/C Pacific, JTWC elsewhere); all storms in the record. Site updated regularly, email me if you see a bug or there is a feature you would like to see added!
